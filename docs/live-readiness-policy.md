# Live-readiness and deliverability policy

Live campaign transport must call the server-side gate immediately before reserving capacity. Both mailbox and domain must be `LIVE_READY`; provider, campaign, suppression, subscription, monthly, mailbox, domain, feature-flag, and Preview gates must pass. All traffic types share the daily capacity ledger. A domain pause blocks every related mailbox and queued work must be held.

Quantum Reach does not guarantee deliverability, inbox placement, replies, or revenue. Capacity depends on health, provider limits, recipient quality, and compliance. Never fabricate opens, replies, or engagement.
