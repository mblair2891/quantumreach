# Revenue OS Foundation

Quantum Reach now has a non-destructive foundation for a full AI Revenue Operating System while preserving the production Zoom/local-recording workflow. The foundation covers CRM extension points, governed CSV imports, AWS SES-first email provider abstraction, suppression/unsubscribe/bounce/complaint enforcement, campaign compliance gates, native scheduling, research-provider abstraction, proposal-to-contract workflows, native e-signature records, Stripe SaaS gating, client workspace provisioning requests, and operator summaries.

Live email is disabled unless `EMAIL_SENDING_ENABLED=true`, `EMAIL_SANDBOX_MODE=false`, AWS SES variables are configured, and the workspace sending domain/sender identity are ready. No scraping or LinkedIn automation is included.
