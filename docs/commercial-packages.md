# Commercial packages

Launch ($297/month + $750 setup), Growth ($597/month + $1,500 setup), and Scale ($997/month + $2,500 setup) are versioned, operator-editable catalog defaults. Every plan includes the complete platform; only capacities and service levels differ. Accepted orders must store the catalog version and terms snapshot so later edits do not rewrite history. Growth is recommended. Mature sending allowances are approximate and become available only after verification and health-based warm-up.

Add-ons are persisted catalog products, never calculation constants: managed domains, domain setup/replacement, mailboxes, domain-plus-three-mailboxes, 5,000 sends, users, contacts, storage, and expedited provisioning. Operators may change active status, price, description, effective date, and assumptions.
